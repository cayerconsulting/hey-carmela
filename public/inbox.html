Hey Carmela tools

Core modular capabilities included in the Hey Carmela business assistant package.

Calls, texts & WhatsApp

Answers incoming phone calls, SMS texts, and WhatsApp messages around the clock so you never lose emergency service jobs.

AI receptionist & staff handoff

Handles routine customer questions, gathers job and address details, and smoothly loops in your staff when a person is needed.

Calendar

Shows live technician availability, working hours, and service territories in real time to prevent double bookings.

Scheduling & reminders

Books and moves jobs on the calendar while dispatching automated text reminders to keep customers informed and eliminate no-shows.

Technician tracking

Sends customers a live GPS tracking link with an accurate estimated arrival time as soon as their technician heads out.

Payments

Tracks money received, collects customer deposits before truck dispatch, and logs payment methods securely.

Billing

Handles post-job invoices and balances, sends itemized receipts, and delivers polite automated follow-ups until paid.

Lead tracking

Records every incoming prospect with their selected priority, language, and date to streamline demo preparation and follow-up.
